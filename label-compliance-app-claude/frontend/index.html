About us

We’re MetrIQ

We build open-source tooling for people who have to get packaging right. MetrIQ started from a simple frustration: checking a label against Indian legal metrology rules meant juggling PDFs of regulations, a magnifying glass, and guesswork. We wanted one tool that reads the pack and points at the exact rule — so inspectors, brands, and small businesses can move faster without trusting a black box.
